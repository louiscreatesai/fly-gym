Fly Gym

A simulated fruit fly doing gym stuff, forever. The body is NeuroMechFly, a 3D model built from a real fly. The brain panel replays real model spikes from a simulation of the fly's whole nervous system (the MaleCNS connectome, 166,700 neurons). Open one and press Brain on a phone, or look right on a computer.

Studio mode (only on this PC). Open a video, press F for full screen and R to start the loop, then film the monitor with your phone in portrait.

Controls

Buttons on the workout card switch cameras, pause and restart. Drag to rotate. On a keyboard: R restart · 1 2 3 cameras · Space pause · F full screen · H hide the panels · C pop-up labels on/off · P fps · G phone framing guide

Filming

  1. Full screen (F), lights low, phone about 30 to 40 cm from the monitor, portrait, the fly strip filling the frame (G shows the two phone frames).
  2. Press R, start recording on the fly. Pan right to the brain panel at the time on the card, hold 2 s, pan back.
  3. In Instagram: the caption in the white Classic font, centred. Sound: Nostalgicore · File Select 64.

How it works

Every flash, count and bar in the brain panel comes from one trial of a real simulation run: a leaky integrate-and-fire model of every neuron in the fly's brain and nerve cord, wired by the connectome, replayed at 0.35x speed and labelled with exactly which neurons were stimulated. It is a model, not a living fly. The gym numbers are jokes.
